The old bakery on Maple Street opens again

By our town reporter · four minutes to read

After two years with its shutters down, the bakery at the corner of Maple and Third lit its ovens again this week. The new owners say they will keep the cinnamon loaf that made the place known across the valley.

Doors open at seven each morning. See the opening hours or find it on the map.
